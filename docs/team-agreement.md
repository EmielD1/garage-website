# Team agreement

How your team works together. Every member reads it at the start, and you read it again when something
goes wrong.

**How to fill in this file**

- Write your own text in the lines and tables under each grey box.
- Replace every TODO, and the hint after it, with your own text. A section that still holds a TODO
  counts as not filled in.
- Delete a line or a table row that you do not use.
- The grey boxes are guidance. The course's checks do not read them, so you may keep them or delete
  them. In the editor, a box is every line from the one that starts with `<table` to the line
  `</td></tr></table>`: to delete a box, delete all of those lines.

## When a pull request is done

<table width="100%"><tr></tr><tr><td>

**Guidance.** A pull request changes the code. A document such as this one needs no pull request:
you commit it straight to `main`. The list below is the course's default. Keep it or change it, and
add your own rules. A teammate's review is optional, so the list says when your team asks for one.

**Example of a rule of your own:** A change to the reservation form is tried once with a full event
before it is merged, because a full event must never accept a reservation.

</td></tr></table>

The author of a pull request merges it when all of this is true:

- The author tried the change on its preview, at a narrow width too when something visible changed.
- Its AI note is filled in.
- The starter's checks have finished. The author read what they say, and fixed what a red check found
  when that was possible.
- A change to the content model has its migration. Only the content-model pair creates migrations.

We ask a teammate for a review when the author is not sure of a change.

## When someone stops contributing

<table width="100%"><tr></tr><tr><td>

**Guidance.** Agree this now, while everyone is calm. One line for each of the three points below is
enough. The last line is the course's default: keep it or change it.

**Example:** If a member does not answer for three working days, their pair partner contacts them
directly. If a task is still open a week after its date, we agree a new date at the weekly meeting
and, if needed, a smaller task. If it happens a second time, we share that member's tasks out again
and update this agreement.
